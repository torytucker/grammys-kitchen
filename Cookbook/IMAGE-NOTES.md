# Recipe pictures

The active cookbook contains illustrative food pictures for Deanna’s Sugar Cookies, Orange Juice Cookies, Pancakes, Quick Mix Spritz, and Slow Cooker Herb Bread. Source recipe photos remain accessible from each recipe footer. Original photos are distinct from generated food images.

Full generated images and prompts for the earlier proof-of-concept batch remain in the unchanged November 6 master archive. The active batch image-provenance file contains only the two retained November 6 images: Deanna’s Sugar Cookies and Slow Cooker Herb Bread. Other active cover images were made during the earlier prototype.
